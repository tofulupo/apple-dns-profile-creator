    Signature

    Requirements for Keychain signing

    A certificate together with its private key in your Keychain: from your organisation, from an Apple Developer account, or made yourself in Keychain Access › Certificate Assistant. Expired certificates are not listed.

    Devices show the profile as “Verified” only if they trust the certificate’s issuer.

    The first time you sign, macOS asks whether security may use the key. Choose Allow, or Always Allow to not be asked again.

    The profile is signed with a certificate from your Keychain; its private key never leaves it. Whether iOS and macOS show it as “Verified” depends on whether they trust that certificate.

    When unsigned, iOS and macOS label it “Not Signed” and ask you to confirm. Use Desktop App for local signing.
